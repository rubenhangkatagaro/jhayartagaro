Capstone Project

BLIND STA: Blind Stick Assistance

◎

About the Project

BLIND STA is a smart blind stick assistance project designed to help visually impaired individuals identify obstacles while moving around.

◉

Project Objectives

  • Detect obstacles before physical contact.
  • Provide audio alerts to warn the user.
  • Support safer movement indoors and outdoors.
  • Develop a lightweight and practical assistive device.
▦

Hardware and Components

  • ESP32 microcontroller
  • HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor
  • DFPlayer Mini audio module
  • Mini speaker
  • Power bank and connecting wires

BLIND STA — Project Documentation

⚙

How It Works

The ultrasonic sensor measures the distance between the stick and nearby objects. The ESP32 processes the sensor readings and triggers an audio alert when an obstacle is detected within the configured distance.
